I'm a CCAPP Postdoctoral Fellow at the Ohio State University Center for Cosmology and AstroParticle Physics (CCAPP). I'm a theorist+observer aiming to uncover the mysteries of galaxy formation probed by star clusters and stellar streams. Click here to learn more about my research.

我是俄亥俄州立大学宇宙学和天体粒子物理学中心的博士后。我通过理论+观测的方法研究星团和星流。这些古老的恒星集团记录了星系早期的信息。科研详情请点击这里。

Before coming to Ohio, I received my Ph.D. from the University of Michigan and B.S. degree from Peking University. In free time, I'm an amateur deep-sky photographer. I also enjoy rock climbing, weightlifting, and exploring new restaurants. If you're reading this page from a conference, feel free to invite me to hang out, work out, or eat out.

在加入俄亥俄之前，我分别于北京大学和密歇根大学获得了学士和博士学位。在闲暇时间，我对深空摄影也略知一二。如果不那么闲暇，我也喜欢攀岩、举铁、探店。如果你在正在一场学术会议里刷这个网页，那尽管邀请我去攀一把、练一下、搓一顿。
